Currently enrolled in the doctoral program at Sogang University. Earned a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Handong University and a Master's degree in Computer Science from Sogang University. The main area of research is speech recognition, having conducted 12 related projects during enrollment and published 14 papers, including 3 SCI-level papers, as the first author. Contributed to open-source projects related to speech recognition such as HuggingFace, ESPNet, Kaldi, and SGSpeech, and conducted 15 advanced/specialist course lectures and consultations on speech recognition. Obtained a doctorate degree by proposing a method to combine speech recognition and language models using WFST.
