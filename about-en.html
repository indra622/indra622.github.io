AI Developer at Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance. My current work starts with the people who actually do the job: I sit with them, learn how they work, and turn that into requirements and decision criteria a system can follow.

I also teach AI internally, including Claude Code sessions for executives.

Previously developed real-time interpretation systems and RAG-based financial advisory chatbots at KB Kookmin Bank Financial AI Center.

Ph.D. in Computer Science from Sogang University, specializing in speech recognition. Proposed a novel method for integrating speech recognition with language models using WFST. Published 14 first-author papers (3 SCI-indexed).

Contributed to HuggingFace, ESPNet, Kaldi, and SGSpeech, and delivered 15 expert-level courses on speech recognition.

Writing the '직접 쓰는 AI교양' series on Threads.
